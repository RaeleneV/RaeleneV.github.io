About

I'm a final-year Information Science student at the University of Pretoria with strengths in systems analysis, information architecture, project leadership, and digital design. I translate complex requirements into organised, user-focused solutions — and I consistently step into leadership roles across team projects.

My academic path is intentionally broad. I began in Multimedia — developing skills in web development, visual design, and digital publishing — before transitioning into Informatics, where I focus on systems analysis, database management, and knowledge management at an organisational level.

Outside of university, I manage operations and documentation for a small family business, and I led a volunteer team delivering computer literacy education to high school students in rural communities.

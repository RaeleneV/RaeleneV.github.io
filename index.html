About

I'm a final-year Information Science student at the University of Pretoria with strengths in systems analysis, information architecture, project leadership, and digital design. I translate complex requirements into organised, user-focused solutions — and I consistently step into leadership roles across team projects.

Over six years my academic path moved through Multimedia, Computer Science, and into Informatics — developing skills in web development, visual design, digital publishing, systems analysis, database management, and knowledge management along the way.

My interests outside of work include painting, reading fiction, web design, and continuous learning — I've independently completed certificates in Tax and Cyber Security outside of my degree.
